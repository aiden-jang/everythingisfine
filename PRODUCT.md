# meanwhile.

## Purpose

A public Earth, ocean and sky observatory. People explore current observations,
forecasts and historical records through one shared globe and map and cinematic
stories. It is also Aiden Jang's showcase of design and technical craft.

## Audience and experience

Curious visitors should understand the invitation immediately, find a story or
layer without setup, and return to their place after exploring it. Support phones,
desktop computers, keyboards and reduced motion. The product is free and keyless.

## Capabilities and constraints

Preserve the shared renderer, real source data, existing cinematic journeys,
source coverage and observation times. Earth, Below the Surface and After Dark
are established scene names. Retain shared links, local saved views, captures,
playback and source recovery. Do not invent observations or require registration.

Most feeds load in the browser. The limited anonymous Worker routes and location
policy are documented in CONVENTIONS.md and SYSTEM.md. Backend or paid-provider
changes need a separate decision. Preferences stay on the visitor's device.

## Design commitments

The approved Aperture direction puts the planet first, with distinctive display
typography, quiet controls and continuous camera-led navigation. It must feel
cinematic, tasteful and responsive without replacing the existing stories or
reducing the scientific information. Studio references describe a standard of
craft; the product has its own visual identity.
